Tone Audition

Prototype for #45. The question: on a real keyboard, is each candidate Tone recognisable, and does it respond quickly enough to play comfortably? Pick a Tone and play it. Velocity, sustain pedal and pitch bend all work. Readouts show which sample answered, the voice count against the 32-voice cap, what the Tone costs in memory, and how long a key takes to sound.

Throwaway. Nothing here is production code. Samples are the original FLAC/WAV files. The MP3 figures are estimates at 128 kbps stereo.

Latency lab

Not armed

The test listens through the built-in microphone with echo cancellation off. Pick Test click, then strike one key sharply. The mic hears the key bottom out, and then the speaker. The gap between the two is the latency, plus or minus 1–2 ms of air. For wired output, hold one earcup to the mic. Check that both markers sit on the right spikes.
