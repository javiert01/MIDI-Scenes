Prototype for How loud is each Tone, and do Tones share a reverb? (built on #45's audition page). The question: what fixed gain does each Tone get, balanced against Warm Analog at a typical velocity, and is reverb shared, per Tone, or none? Also: fine-tune Villera Lead D, settle the Rock Organ, set the guitar's EQ tilt, and check every Tone at C2 and B6. Samples are prepared in the browser to the settings of #49.

Loudness balance

Tone Loudness before gain Suggested gain Gain now Phrase peak at output

Each measurement renders the same phrase offline (C3, C4, C5, then a C4–E4–G4 triad) at the reference velocity, with no reverb, and reads its approximate K-weighted gated loudness. The suggested gain matches Warm Analog. It's a starting point: settle each gain by ear.

The test listens through the built-in microphone with echo cancellation off. Pick Test click, then strike one key sharply. The mic hears the key bottom out, and then the speaker. The gap between the two is the latency, plus or minus 1–2 ms of air. For wired output, hold one earcup to the mic. Check that both markers sit on the right spikes.
